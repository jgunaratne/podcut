## Description

**Stop listening to 2-hour podcasts when you only need the highlights.**

Podcut is the podcast player that actually respects your time. Search, listen, transcribe, and summarize any episode — all in one app.

**🎧 Listen Your Way**
Stream any podcast with background playback, lock screen controls, and adjustable speed from 0.5x to 2x. Save your favorites for quick access.

**📝 On-Device Transcription**
Tap once to transcribe a full episode. Every word gets a timecode — tap any timestamp to jump right to that moment. All processing happens on your device. No audio ever leaves your phone.

**✨ AI-Powered Summaries**
Get a concise, timestamped breakdown of any episode in seconds. See exactly what was discussed and when, with tappable timecodes to dive deeper.

**💬 Chat With Episodes**
Got a question about something discussed in the episode? Ask it. Podcut's AI chat gives you answers grounded in the actual transcript — no hallucinations, no guessing.

**🔒 Privacy First**
Transcription is 100% on-device using Apple's Speech framework. Your audio never touches a server. AI summaries use the text only, never the audio.

**What's Free:**
• Search and discover millions of podcasts
• Full audio playback with lock screen controls
• Complete on-device transcription with timecodes
• Favorites, playback speed, audio caching

**Podcut Pro ($2.99/mo):**
• AI-powered timestamped summaries
• Chat with any episode
• Regenerate summaries anytime
